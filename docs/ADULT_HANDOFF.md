# V3 — Adult/Authorized Account Handoff

**No purchase, account, contract, or real customer data is currently authorized by this document.**

## What exists
The GitHub repository contains a local Python catalog-validation engine, a separate Django demonstration portal, simulated store accounts, operator review controls, audit events, independent QA, and automated test workflows. The service is **not production-ready** and has no actual paying customers.

## What should wait for an authorized adult/legal signatory
- Confirm appropriate business structure, ownership and any required registrations for a minor-led business
- Open/verify any hosting, domain, payment processor, or banking account whose terms require an adult
- Review and sign customer contracts, privacy/data-processing terms and payment obligations as necessary
- Approve a written spending cap and who can authorize charges
- Arrange qualified legal/accounting/security advice where appropriate
- Authorize real customer data processing only after security review and a defined retention policy

## Accounts or secrets NOT to share with the AI
Never give the AI passwords, payment card numbers, passkeys, bank sign-in details, secret API keys, or identity documents in a prompt. Use secure provider configuration and direct human authorization.

## Minimum spending decision
Before any charges, decide:
- Maximum startup spending allowed
- Maximum recurring monthly infrastructure cost
- Who owns and can cancel each subscription
- How cancellation or a failed launch is handled

No claim of projected revenue can justify unapproved spending.

## Technical go/no-go
An experienced reviewer should sign off on hosting security, authentication, tenant isolation, database/file encryption, backup restoration, incident response, service terms, data deletion, and user acceptance tests. Local automated tests are not substitutes.
