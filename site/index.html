The problem

Small data mistakes create expensive headaches.

Unexpected updates

One supplier file can change prices, titles or inventory unintentionally.

Manual checking

Reviewing hundreds of rows takes time that retailers need elsewhere.

Unclear accountability

Alerts alone still leave someone figuring out what went wrong.

Proposed service

Reviewed changes, not another spreadsheet chore.

Provide files

Share your existing catalog and supplier update through an approved secure workflow.

Automated checks

Rules identify duplicates, invalid values and unusual changes.

Human oversight

Important or ambiguous cases stay on hold until reviewed.

Verified output

Receive a checked, import-ready CSV and a clear exception report.

Pilot status: V3 is in preproduction testing. No customer data, payment, live integration or service-level guarantee is being accepted. Features and pricing will be finalized after controlled validation.
